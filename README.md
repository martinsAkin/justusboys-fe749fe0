# Justusboys

# PROJECT: JUST US BOYS AFRICA

## Complete Website Redesign & Development

Act as a senior product designer, creative director, and experienced frontend developer.

I want you to completely redesign and build the website for **Just Us Boys Africa**, an African men's community initiative focused on connection, personal development, accountability, emotional expression, and brotherhood.

Current website: [https://justusboys.africa/](https://justusboys.africa/)

Visual inspiration: [https://drafted.ng/](https://drafted.ng/)

The goal is to create a visually distinctive, premium, modern, editorial-style website that feels like a movement and a community people genuinely want to belong to.

IMPORTANT: Do not clone Drafted. Use its expressive editorial design language, animation, bold typography, interesting layouts, strong visual hierarchy, and creative use of whitespace as inspiration. Maintain the unique identity and purpose of Just Us Boys.

# 1. BRAND IDENTITY AND POSITIONING

Brand name: Just Us Boys

Brand location: Africa, with an initial Nigerian/Lagos community presence.

Core positioning:
A community for men to connect, express themselves, find direction, grow, and build meaningful relationships.

Existing brand tagline:

"No Pressure. Just Direction."

Supporting statement:

"Just men building men. A safe space for connection, expression, and conversations that actually matter."

Brand personality:

- Confident, not arrogant.
- Masculine, without reinforcing toxic masculinity.
- Warm, welcoming, and relatable.
- Youthful and culturally relevant.
- Thoughtful, expressive, and community-driven.

The website should make visitors feel that they are entering a community, not visiting a conventional NGO website.

Preserve the existing Just Us Boys brand logo. Use the existing white logo asset where appropriate:

# 2. DESIGN SYSTEM

Create a distinctive editorial visual system.

COLOUR PALETTE:

- Primary background: forest green(#0611OC).
- Accent: Burnt orange (#D96B35) or chrimson red a31c1b.
- Secondary accent: Muted olive or earthy green.
- Text: Deep charcoal black and soft grey.

Use orange sparingly for important highlights, buttons, labels, and visual accents.

TYPOGRAPHY:

Use a bold contemporary display typeface such as Space Grotesk, Archivo, nova-square or a similar expressive sans-serif.

Pair it with a clean readable sans-serif for paragraphs and navigation.

Use:

- Oversized headings.
- Strong typographic contrasts.
- Short editorial statements.
- Large section titles.
- Generous whitespace.

DESIGN LANGUAGE:

- Editorial magazine layouts.
- Asymmetric grid systems.
- Large photographic compositions.
- Layered image collages.
- Oversized typography.
- Subtle grain and texture.
- Rounded elements used selectively.
- Strong dark/light section contrasts.
- Smooth scrolling and restrained motion.
- Interesting section transitions.

Avoid:

- Generic NGO templates.
- Excessive rounded cards.
- Excessive gradients.
- Generic corporate illustrations.
- Too many floating elements.
- Repetitive three-column card layouts.
- Overly complicated animations.
